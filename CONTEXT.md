# Tandem

Tandem helps exactly two partners record and understand the expenses they manage together.

## Language

**Partner**:
One of the two people managing shared expenses in Tandem.
_Avoid_: User, profile, member

**Shared expense**:
An expense both partners are responsible for, divided using either the default split or a custom split.
_Avoid_: Joint transaction

**Personal expense**:
An expense assigned entirely to the partner who paid it. It contributes to household spending insights but not to the settlement balance.
_Avoid_: No-split expense

**Owed expense**:
An expense one partner paid entirely on behalf of the other partner.
_Avoid_: Beneficiary expense

**Default split**:
The partners’ usual percentages for sharing a shared expense, applied automatically unless they override it.
_Avoid_: Profile split, standard split

**Custom split**:
Percentages chosen for one shared expense instead of the default split.
_Avoid_: Manual split

**Settlement**:
Money transferred from one partner to the other to reduce or clear the settlement balance. It is not spending or income.
_Avoid_: Liquidation, payment

**Settlement balance**:
The all-time amount one partner owes the other after shared expenses, owed expenses, and settlements are accounted for.
_Avoid_: Debt, monthly balance
